# CodeSaver CLI v1.7.6

## New functions

- Report overly long archive member paths and the longest stored path.
- Summarize top-level folders and loose root files in a ZIP archive.
- Inspect archive-level and per-member comments.
- Export a per-file CRC-32 inventory without decompressing archive contents.
- Summarize member timestamps by year and identify the oldest and newest entries.

Released on 29 September 2026 after green validation.
